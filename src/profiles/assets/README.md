# Sign-in page art

`header.webp` and `logo.webp` come from the Recharged launcher
(previouslevelgames/recharged, `assets/launcher/`), so that the pages the
launcher opens look like it.

- `header.webp` (1600 x 889) is the launcher's header at rest: the scene
  (`header_back.png`, grown 1.065 times about Mario's face), the gold strokes
  (`header_strokes.png`) and Mario (`header_front.png`), composited and scaled
  down. It is made from the key art of Mario Strikers Charged, which is
  Nintendo's; rights remain with their owners. The strokes' creator and terms
  are not recorded yet.
- `logo.webp` (720 x 401) is the launcher's `logo.png`, scaled down. Its
  creator and terms are not recorded yet.
